## What it is

`cafe-method` is an **AIOX squad** that transforms any dense book into a **mediated reading trail**: a main post (book overview + club structure) and N sequential comments (each one a dense chapter, faithful to the source, with archetypal layer). Output ready to publish in community — Circle, Discord, Slack, forum, reading group, newsletter.

Born from the real practice of **Café Lendár[IA]** (Lendária Community · AIOX) crossing *The Hero with a Thousand Faces* by Joseph Campbell. The method was distilled, generalized, and packaged as a plug-and-play squad.

**Who it's for:** people interested in extracting what truly matters from dense books — not summary, not TLDR, not motivational tip. **Faithful translation of the source with an actionable archetypal layer.**
